## 2025-05-14 - Asset Optimization for Documentation
**Learning:** Converting high-resolution PNG screenshots to WebP with quality 80 in a documentation-centric repository consistently yields ~85% reduction in asset size with negligible visual quality loss. Adding native lazy loading to non-hero images further improves perceived performance by delaying the download of off-screen assets.
**Action:** Always propose WebP conversion and lazy loading for documentation-heavy repositories as a high-impact, low-risk performance win.
