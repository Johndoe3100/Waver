## 2025-06-15 - Asset Optimization for Documentation-Centric Repos
**Learning:** In repositories primarily composed of documentation (README.md) and assets, image optimization is the most impactful performance win. Converting PNGs to WebP with a quality setting of 80 achieved an ~85% reduction in total asset size with negligible visual quality loss.
**Action:** Always check the `assets/` directory for large PNG/JPEG files in documentation-heavy repositories and propose WebP conversion as a high-impact, low-risk optimization.
